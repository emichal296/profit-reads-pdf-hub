Privacy reminder

How submissions work

  1. A visitor selects a PDF (10 MB or less).
  2. The form checks that it is a PDF and displays its size.
  3. Once you connect an upload service, the PDF can be sent to your private review area.
  4. You review it before deciding whether to add it to the public library.
