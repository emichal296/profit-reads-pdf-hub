Submit a PDF

PDF submissions

Choose a PDF directly from your computer using File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac), then submit it through Google Forms. You do not need to put it in your own Google Drive first. Google stores submissions in the form owner's Drive. A Google Account sign-in is required.

How submissions work

  1. A visitor clicks “Open PDF submission form.”
  2. They sign in to Google if prompted and choose a PDF.
  3. Google Forms stores the submitted file in the form owner’s Google Drive.
  4. You review the PDF and decide whether it belongs in the public library.
